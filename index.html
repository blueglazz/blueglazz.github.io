About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Mathematical Sciences at Tsinghua University. My advisor is Zhengwei Liu.

Research Interests

My research focuses on operator algebras, in particular subfactor theory, and their applications in topological orders and quantum information theory. I use mathematical tools stemming operator algebras to understand quantum phases of matter.
