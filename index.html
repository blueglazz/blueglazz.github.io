About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Mathematical Sciences at Tsinghua University. My advisor is Zhengwei Liu.

Research Interests

My research focuses on operator algebras, in particular subfactor theory, quantum information theory, and topological orders. I use mathematical tools from operator algebras and quantum information to understand quantum phases of matter.
