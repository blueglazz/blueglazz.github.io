About Me

I am a Postdoctoral researcher in Mathematics. Starting in Fall 2026, I am joining Janet Hung's group at Yau Mathematical Sciences Center, Tsinghua University.

Research Interests

My research focuses on operator algebras, quantum information theory, and topological orders. I use mathematical tools from operator algebras and quantum information to study quantum phases of matter.
